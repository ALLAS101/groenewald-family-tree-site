The Groenewald line in South Africa

Proven, generation by generation. Each Groenewald parent–child link from step 4 down to step 7 is backed by a baptism or death-notice record. Steps 1–3 come from the standard, record-based Cape genealogies.

  • 1 · Christoph Grünwald Documented
    before c.1667 – 1721 · Königsberg → Hoorn 1685 → the Cape by 1687
    A VOC corporal. Married Catharina Niemand of Amsterdam in Cape Town in 1691. He's the stamvader of the South African Groenewalds.
  • 2 · Coenraad Groenewald Documented
    c.1698 · Stellenbosch
    Married Catharina Loos in 1719.
  • 3 · Coenraad Groenewalt Documented
    bapt. 1731 – d. 1813 · Stellenbosch

  • 4 · Coenraad Adolph Groenewald Documented
    bapt. 14 Sep 1788, Stellenbosch · d. 3 May 1840, Klipdrift, Napier
    Born on the farm Zeekoejagt near Riviersonderend. He's the first "Coenraad Adolph" found in the records. Married Susara Johanna Matthee in 1810.
  • 5 · Casparus Johannes Groenewald Documented
    c.1830 – 1914 · Klippedrift, Bredasdorp
    Married Maria Elizabeth Christina van Dyk (1838–1915).
  • 6 · Coenraad Adolph Groenewald Documented
    b. 27 Feb 1857 · bapt. 19 Apr 1857, Swellendam · d. 22 May 1936, Wepener
    Your great-great-grandfather. His baptism record names his parents as Casparus Johannes Groenewald and Maria Elizabeth Christina van Dyk (step 5), and the birth date matches his gravestone. His first wife, Anna Louisa Sophia Swart (m. 1882), died in 1901. In 1908 he married Magdalena Sophia Maria Cox (1886–1960) in Wepener. The family moved back and forth between the Free State and the southern Cape. Their children included Coenraad Adolf (b. 1912, Wepener), Johanna Dorothea (b. 1913, Hobhouse), Michiel William (b. 1918, Napier), Maria Elizabeth (b. 1920, Napier), Amelia Elizabeth (b. 1922, Bredasdorp), Pieter Andries (b. 1923, Riversdale), William John (b. 1926), Dorothea William (b. 1927) and Paulina Susanna Carolina (b. 1929, Wepener).
  • 7 · Pieter Andries Groenewald Documented
    b. 29 Nov 1923 · bapt. 20 Aug 1924, Riversdale (NG Kerk) · m. 19 Nov 1943, Grasland, Bloemfontein
    Your great-grandfather. He was a railway worker at 19 when he married Dorothea Magdalena Cox (b. 27 Feb 1926, Wepener). His mother Magdalena Sophia Maria Cox and Dorothea's father Thomas Jesaja Cox were brother and sister, so the couple were first cousins. That was legal, and not unusual in farming communities at the time.
  • 8 · Coenraad Adolf Groenewald Known
    1944 – 2026 · Free State → Randfontein
    Your grandfather, named after his grandfather (step 6), following the naming custom. Married Susanna Maria van der Walt. Served on the border, and ran his own farm-equipment repair business, Agri Services. Brother of Pieter Andries.

Your generation, in short

You were born in 2003, so you're part of Generation Z. Your grandfather was born in 1944, during the Second World War, which puts him in the Silent Generation, right at the edge of the baby boomers.

Counting back through the Groenewald name, you're the 10th generation since Christoph Grünwald reached the Cape (by 1687), and the 9th born in South Africa. Every step is now backed by records. Through Grandma Susanna, you also descend from the van der Walts, who have been in South Africa for about 9–10 generations.
